THE MODEL

What is calculated?

Pressure = 101.325 kPa × remaining fraction. Oxygen partial pressure = 0.2095 × pressure. Remaining original air mass = 5.1 × 10¹⁸ kg × remaining fraction. Water vapor pressure is approximated using the Antoine equation.

Gravity and air composition are held fixed. Water vapor from evaporation, atmospheric circulation, changing temperature, radiation dose and local weather are not simulated. Sky color, escaping particles and cloud opacity are illustrative, not quantitative predictions. The habitat is an independent pressure demonstration, not a safety assessment.
